Check Printing Format

Design and print your own check layout directly from Odoo. Set exact coordinates for the date, partner, amount, amount in words and voucher detail, and print checks straight from your vendor payments.

Step 1 - Create a check format

Go to Accounting/Invoicing > Configuration > Check Print and create a new format.

Step 2 - Configure date, partner, amount and voucher, then set it to Done

Enable the blocks you need (Date, Partner, Amount, Amount in letters, Not Negotiable, Voucher) and set the position (top/left/width/height in cm), style and format of each one to match your physical check paper. Click "Set Done" to generate the printable report, its QWeb view and paper format automatically - no XML/QWeb editing required.

Step 3 - Assign the format to a journal

In Configuration > Journals, open your bank journal and select the "Check Printing Format" you just created.

Step 4 - Print the check from the payment

Post a vendor payment with the "Checks" payment method and click "Print Check". The PDF is generated with your custom layout and the payment is marked as sent.
